PROJECT · P.02 — URBAN RAIL

Metro depot coordination

Year2023
RoleBIM Coordinator
ToolsNavisworks · BIM 360 · QA/QC
HighlightClash matrix · On-time meetings

A depot packs maintenance shops, tracks and MEP systems into an extremely tight site. We ran a structured clash matrix in Navisworks — which discipline pair tests which, at what tolerance, owned by whom — all agreed upfront.

Every issue lived on BIM 360 with a deadline and attached snapshots. So coordination meetings existed to approve decisions, not to hunt for problems — and they always ended on time.

300+Clashes closed pre-IFC
12Discipline pairs in matrix
100%Coordination meetings on time

Outcomes

  • Hundreds of clashes closed before construction issue — nothing pending carried into the build phase.
  • The clash matrix became a template the team still reuses on new projects.
  • A stable coordination cadence: clear topics, decisions with owners, meetings always ending on time.
